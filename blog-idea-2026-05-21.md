## 2. Hook / Summary

Cache invalidation is famously one of the two hard problems in computer science — and for headless CMS platforms running at the edge, it's where most architectures quietly fall apart. This post breaks down how SonicJs combines Cloudflare KV, tag-based purging, and a stale-while-revalidate strategy to serve content in single-digit milliseconds *and* reflect editorial updates near-instantly, without the origin-fetch storms that plague traditional CMS caching.

## 3. Suggested Outline

1. **The real problem:** Why edge caching for CMS content is deceptively hard — the tension between TTL-based freshness and sub-10ms reads, and why naive cache busting causes thundering-herd origin fetches across hundreds of PoPs.
2. **The SonicJs cache model:** How content (flat-file or DB-backed) gets normalized, serialized, and written to Cloudflare KV at the edge, with cache keys derived from content type + entry + locale.
3. **Tag-based purging in practice:** Walk through associating content entries with cache tags, and surgically purging only affected keys when an editor publishes — instead of nuking the whole cache.
4. **Stale-while-revalidate:** Serving the last-known-good version instantly while revalidating in the background via Cloudflare Workers `waitUntil()`, so readers never wait on a cold fetch.
5. **Measuring it:** Before/after latency and origin-load numbers; what p50/p99 look like with and without SWR.
6. **When NOT to cache this way:** Personalized content, preview mode, and the escape hatches SonicJs provides for bypassing the edge cache.
