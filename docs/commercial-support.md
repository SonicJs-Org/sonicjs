# Commercial Support

> Sample pricing and SLA figures below reflect industry norms for open-source headless-CMS support. Confirm final numbers before publishing.

SonicJS is, and always will be, **100% MIT open source** — every feature ships in the core, with no Growth tier and no Enterprise feature gate.

Commercial support does **not** change that. It exists for one reason: to give teams a **paid support contract with a guaranteed response time and a named contact** so that adopting SonicJS clears internal procurement, security, and vendor-risk review.

If you're a developer who wants to advocate for SonicJS but your company requires "vendor support" before approving a dependency — this page is what you forward to your manager or procurement team.

---

## What you're buying (and what you're not)

**You are buying:**

- A **response-time SLA** you can put in front of procurement.
- A **private channel** (email / Slack) instead of public issues.
- A **named human** who knows your deployment.
- **Priority security triage** and upgrade/architecture guidance.
- (Enterprise) **license indemnification** for legal/compliance sign-off.

**You are not buying features.** Every capability in SonicJS is in the open-source core under MIT and always will be. Support is additive, never a gate.

## Tiers

### Community — Free
- All features (MIT, forever)
- GitHub Issues, Community Discussions, Discord
- Best-effort, community-driven response

### Priority Support — `$499/mo` (billed annually)
Everything in Community, plus:
- **Guaranteed response: next business day** (Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM ET)
- Private **email** support channel
- **Priority security-issue triage**
- Upgrade and architecture guidance
- Best for: startups and teams running SonicJS in production that need a support paper trail.

### Enterprise — from `$2,500/mo`
Everything in Priority, plus:
- **Guaranteed response: same business day** (1-business-hour target for Sev-1)
- Private **email + Slack** channel
- **Named support contact** familiar with your deployment
- **License indemnification / IP warranty**
- Optional: onboarding, custom migration help, security questionnaire support (SOC2/vendor forms)
- Best for: enterprises with procurement, legal, or security review requirements.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)

| Severity | Definition | Priority target | Enterprise target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sev-1** | Production down / data loss | 4 business hours | 1 business hour |
| **Sev-2** | Major feature broken, no workaround | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| **Sev-3** | Minor bug, workaround exists | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| **Sev-4** | Question / guidance | Best effort | 2 business days |

- **Support hours:** 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM ET (UTC−5), Monday–Friday, excluding US public holidays.
- **Response** = a human engaging with your ticket, not resolution time.
- SLA applies to the **latest minor** and one prior minor release.

## For procurement & security teams

Common vendor-review questions, answered:

- **License:** MIT. No copyleft obligations. [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
- **Source availability:** Full source public on GitHub — no vendor lock-in; support lapse never removes access to the software.
- **Support entity:** SonicJS, Inc. (contracting party — confirm final legal entity).
- **Indemnification:** Available on the Enterprise tier.
- **Security reporting:** See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) *(create if missing)*; Priority/Enterprise get priority triage.
- **Data residency:** SonicJS runs on **your** Cloudflare account — you own the data and infrastructure. SonicJS the vendor holds no customer data.
- **Business continuity:** Because the software is MIT and self-hosted on your infrastructure, a lapse in support or vendor availability does not interrupt your running deployment.
