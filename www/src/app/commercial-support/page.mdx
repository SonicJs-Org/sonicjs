# Commercial Support

Every SonicJS feature is, and always will be, **100% MIT open source** — no Growth tier, no Enterprise feature gate. Commercial support does not change that. It gives your team a **paid support contract with a guaranteed response time and a named contact**, so adopting SonicJS clears procurement, security, and vendor-risk review. {{ className: 'lead' }}

If you want to advocate for SonicJS but your company requires "vendor support" before approving a dependency — this is the page you forward to your manager or procurement team.

## What you get {{ id: 'what-you-get' }}

**You are buying:**

- A **response-time SLA** you can put in front of procurement.
- A **private channel** (email / Slack) instead of public issues.
- A **named human** who knows your deployment.
- **Priority security triage** and upgrade/architecture guidance.
- (Enterprise) **license indemnification** for legal/compliance sign-off.

**You are not buying features.** Every capability ships in the open-source core under MIT and always will. Support is additive, never a gate.

Everything in Community, plus guaranteed next-business-day response, private email channel, priority security triage, and upgrade/architecture guidance. Best for startups and teams running SonicJS in production that need a support paper trail.

## Service Level Agreement {{ id: 'sla' }}

| Severity | Definition | Priority target | Enterprise target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sev-1** | Production down / data loss | 4 business hours | 1 business hour |
| **Sev-2** | Major feature broken, no workaround | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| **Sev-3** | Minor bug, workaround exists | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| **Sev-4** | Question / guidance | Best effort | 2 business days |

- **Support hours:** 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM ET (UTC−5), Mon–Fri, excluding US public holidays.
- **Response** = a human engaging with your ticket, not resolution time.
- SLA applies to the latest minor release and one prior minor.

## For procurement & security teams {{ id: 'procurement' }}

- **License:** MIT. No copyleft obligations.
- **Source availability:** Full source public on GitHub — no vendor lock-in. A support lapse never removes access to the software.
- **Indemnification:** Available on the Enterprise tier.
- **Data residency:** SonicJS runs on **your** Cloudflare account — you own the data and infrastructure. The vendor holds no customer data.
- **Business continuity:** Because the software is MIT and self-hosted on your infrastructure, a lapse in support or vendor availability does not interrupt your running deployment.
